HOW TO PLAY

Controls

  • Space, click, or tap — jump
  • R — restart the level you're on
  • Esc, or the Menu button — back to level select

Obstacles

  • Spike — touch it and you explode, level restarts.
  • Block — land on top to run across it safely. Hit the side and you explode.
  • Both can also appear upside-down on the ceiling once you hit a gravity flip — same rules, flipped.

Portals

  • Gravity Flip — flips what's "down" for you. The ceiling becomes the floor until the next one.
  • Speed Portal — changes how fast you're moving, faster or slower.
  • Godzilla pickup (in custom levels) — touch it to smash every block in the level in one hit.

Levels

  • Beginner — a short, gentle warm-up. Start here if you're new.
  • Levels 1–10 — the main run, climbing from Easy to Hard.
  • Pro — the longest level in the game. It looks brutal, but it's actually pretty fair if you take it steady — the challenge is going the distance, not tricky jumps.

Level Maker

Build and save your own levels — completely unlimited. Find it from the main menu, under "Level Maker."

Skins

Unlock new looks for your cube by beating levels, chaining spike dodges without dying, or finding secrets. Pick your favorite from the "Skins" menu.

Secrets

There are a few cheat codes hidden in this game — just type them on your keyboard while playing. Good luck finding them. 😉
